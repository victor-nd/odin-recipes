Sausage balls

Description

cripsy delicious sausage balls that's made u of sharp cheddar and pork sausage that delivers rich savoury flavour with each bite

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground pork sausage, at room temperature
  • 2 cups biscuit baking mix
  • 12 ounces sharp Cheddar cheese, shredded

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Combine sausage and biscuit mix in a large bowl; mix with your hands until evenly combined. Add Cheddar and continue mixing until the mixture holds together. It may seem dry at first, but there’s no need to add liquid.
  3. Shape the mixture into about 30 balls, each about 1 ½ inches wide, and arrange them on a rimmed baking sheet.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown and an instant-read thermometer inserted into the centers registers 160 degrees F (71 degrees C), 20 to 25 minutes.
